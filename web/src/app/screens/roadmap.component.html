What the product is

Lease a machine. Settle it on the chain.

A person with a machine, one GB10 or a rack, lists it. A person who needs it leases it. Nobody has to invest to participate, and nobody has to trust the other side.

  1. 1

    List a machine

    Flash the Slotfare image, and the machine registers itself. The on-ramp withholds a share of earned revenue instead of asking for capital, so a machine and no cash is enough to start.

  2. 2

    Lease it with escrow

    Credits go into an escrow the chain holds. The provider is paid per epoch against evidence it did not author alone. A lease that stops delivering stops paying.

  3. 3

    Connect with your key

    Your SSH key, over a private tunnel that only the active lease can open. When the lease ends, the machine reboots clean and your data is gone by design.

The lease, concretely

The whole machine, not an API on it

Your key is the credential

You generate an SSH key pair in the browser at lease time. The public key goes into the lease record on chain. Nothing else authorizes access.

The tunnel is the second wall

The provider agent opens a private tunnel for the active lease only, using tailcat: Tailscale’s data plane with no control plane, no accounts, and no server that could add a peer. The chain is the only place a peer gets authorized.

The image does the work

A NixOS image with a pinned flake: the agent, tailcat, the SSH server and nothing else. Reproducible by construction, so its measurement can be published.

The machine is ephemeral

Lease end drops the peer, wipes the key and reboots into a clean generation. Data on a leased machine is gone by design.

The mainnet gate

Conditions, not a date

  • All four phases run on a public testnet.
  • At least one provider and one lessee who are not the authors have completed a lease.
  • The open problems list is published unchanged. Nothing on it is claimed solved, and the dispute adjudicator stays named as unsolved.
  • Credits at launch are prepaid with no exit door. The standing bid ships with cross-chain settlement, and mainnet does not wait for it.
Named, not hidden

What stays trusted, and what stays unsolved

The relay

When a direct path fails, the tunnel falls back to a relay. It is encrypted end to end, so the relay cannot read or inject, but it can be unavailable. Providers at the 99.9 percent tier and above must be directly reachable.

The adjudicator

Nobody in this sector has decentralized who decides a dispute. The design caps per-lease exposure and invalidates evidence under deterministic rules, and leaves the adjudicator on the open problems list.

The full list, with every rejected design and the reason, is kept in the project’s decision log.
